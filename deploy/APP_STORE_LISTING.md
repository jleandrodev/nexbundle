## 9. Instruções de teste para o revisor (campo "Testing instructions")

```
Test store login: [INFORMAR loja de teste + acesso, se aplicável]

Steps to review NexBundle:
1. Install the app on a development store. Complete OAuth (scope: read_products).
2. In the app, open "Components" → "Create component". Pick a main product and one
   or more companion products, choose a layout (side by side / list / compact) and Save.
3. In the Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes → Customize. On a product template,
   add the app block "NexBundle — Side by side" (or Compact) to the page and Save.
4. Open that product page in the storefront: the buy-together block appears with both
   products and an "add both to cart" button. Clicking it adds both via the native cart.
5. Back in the app, open "Analytics" to see impressions/clicks/CTR.
6. Billing: open "Plans" and start a subscription (test charge). The 7-day trial applies.
7. Language: switch the admin panel language (auto by Shopify locale + manual selector on
   the dashboard). Admin and storefront strings follow PT-BR / EN / ES.
8. GDPR: uninstalling the app triggers app/uninstalled; shop/redact (~48h) deletes all
   shop data. Compliance webhooks are HMAC-verified.

Support: suporte@nexbundle.sprezzia.live  ·  Privacy: /legal/privacy  ·  Terms: /legal/terms
```
